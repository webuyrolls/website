PSA Materials

Pressure sensitive adhesive materials for the label and tape industries — self-wound and supported rolls.

Pressure Sensitive Adhesive (PSA) materials are primarily used in the label and tape industries. If you have PSA-backed materials, we are interested in self-wound and supported rolls.

Regardless of your application, we are able to provide a ready home for most surplus and overstock tapes and labels. We can also use many types of PSA-backed metals and fabrics — just send us the details.

We buy

  • Medical tape
  • Heat shrink tape
  • Paper tape
  • Metalized film labels
  • Label stock, slit & master rolls
  • Plastic tape BOPP, PE & PET
  • Double sided tapes
  • Silicone release liner
