01 Materials

A ready home for your surplus roll stock.

Overrun, outdated, damaged or rejected, printed or unprinted. If it's on a core, send us the details.

Aluminum foil

Bare, lacquered or laminated. Aluminum foil is an essential component in food and pharmaceutical packaging, and we take surplus or damaged roll stock of laminated or uncoated foil substrates. We also provide guaranteed destruction for sensitive printed foil and laminates.

We buy

  • Aluminum foil rolls
  • Poly coated lid stock
  • Heat sealable laminated foil
  • Pouch
  • Butter foil
  • Laminated cable wrap
  • Gum wrap
  • Lidding foil
  • Blister pack foil
  • Cold form foil
  • Cigarette foil
  • PSA foil tape
  • Paper foil
  • Candy foil

Plastic film

Our main interest is single-resin, unlaminated film rolls: PP, PET, LDPE, HDPE, PVC or nylon. We can also use many types of laminate films, including pouch and lidding structures. Just send us the details.

We buy

  • Nylon films
  • Heat shrink & Cryovac
  • Blister card PVC, PET
  • Metallized film rolls
  • PET films, clear and colors
  • BOPP, OPP, PP films
  • Heat sealable pouch films
  • Polyethylene HD, LD & LLD

Paper

Unprinted rolls of thin-gauge paper are our main interest. Colors are fine, as are natural and bleached paper and board, kraft or bleached, tissue through board grades. Coated or laminated one or two sides is also in demand.

We buy

  • Wet strength
  • Silicone coated
  • Metallized paper
  • Poly coated
  • White and natural kraft
  • Tissue, white and colors
  • Thermal print paper
  • Board, white 1S or 2S

Vacuum deposit & metallized

Metallized plastic film or paper, laminated or coated, printed or unprinted. That includes holographic and hot stamping foil from metallizers and converters.

We buy

  • Metallized film
  • Metallized paper
  • Laminated / coated
  • Holographic foil
  • Hot stamping foil
  • Printed rollstock
  • Unprinted rollstock

Nonwoven fabric

Nonwovens are essential in the medical, automotive and personal care industries, and they're developing fast, so send us all the particulars and we'll come back promptly with a quote. Large quantities will not scare us.

We buy

  • Spunbond PP / PET / PS / PE
  • Spunlace
  • Air laid materials
  • Hydrophobic fabric
  • Hydrophilic fabric
  • Medical garment laminates
  • All types of wipes
  • Filter materials

Pressure sensitive adhesive

PSA materials for the label and tape industries, in self-wound and supported rolls. We take most surplus and overstock tapes and labels, plus many PSA-backed metals and fabrics.

We buy

  • Medical tape
  • Heat shrink tape
  • Paper tape
  • Metallized film labels
  • Label stock, slit & master rolls
  • BOPP, PE & PET tape
  • Double sided tape
  • Silicone release liner

From full warehouse to cleared floor.
