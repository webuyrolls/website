About Us

CMI, Inc.

CMI, Inc. is an American owned and operated company that has been buying excess packaging inventory for over 35 years. Located in New England, we can pick up trailer loads of material throughout North America. Our expertise is minimizing your disposal and warehousing costs while maximizing the value of your surplus packaging rolls.

We are not consultants, we are hands on recovery experts with our own 35,000 square foot warehouse devoted to overstock packaging materials. Our staff has more than 100 years of combined experience in the flexible packaging industry, and just like you, we are packaging professionals.

We provide pick up service for full and partial loads of your surplus packaging inventory. Just contact us with photos and as much detail as possible and we will get right back with you with a proposal. We can usually have a truck at your warehouse within 48 hours.

In addition, we can provide guaranteed product destruction services for sensitive rolls as well as recycling options for many packaging materials. Simply let us know what you need.
